# World guide: Yūgure

Reference for the GM. The player may read this too; it holds no spoilers. Secrets about the case live in `gm/case-file.md`.

## The two sides

**The south coast.** Present-day England. Alice and Simon's terraced house, the sea front, the police who think Simon simply walked out. Ordinary, grey, a little too quiet now. Normal modern technology works here.

**Yūgure** (夕暮れ, "dusk"). The city on the other side of the door. It feels like a 1980s Japanese metropolis that never quite existed: glass and concrete towers, neon kanji signs stacked up their sides, elevated trains rattling between rooftops, narrow alleys of izakaya and pachinko parlours under strings of paper lanterns, canals reflecting all of it. It is nearly always dusk or night, and it rains often. Whether Yūgure is another world, another time or a pocket the two have made between them is a mystery the story can answer slowly, or never.

## Look and feel

- **80s, not cyberpunk.** No implants, no AI, no internet, no mobile phones. This is not Altered Carbon or Blade Runner. Technology is minimal and analogue: payphones, rotary phones, pagers for the rich, cassette Walkmans, VHS, CRT televisions in shop windows, film cameras, typewriters, fax and telex in offices, vending machines everywhere, taxis with lace seat covers.
- **Neon and rain.** Every scene can have a light source and a reflection, which matters because Alice scrys in reflections.
- **Synth on the radio, city pop in the bars.** Arcades, karaoke boxes, late-night ramen counters, convenience stores glowing at 3 a.m.
- **Old under the new.** Shrines wedged between towers, foxes on the rooftops, lanterns that light themselves. Magic is real here and older than the city, but most people would rather not talk about it.
- **The city is haunted.** Ghosts are part of Yūgure's everyday life: yūrei who linger where they died, a payphone that rings for the dead, a karaoke booth nobody books after midnight, faces in the canal that aren't reflections. Some ghosts want help, some want company, a few want something worse. With Witch's Sight, Alice notices them where most people walk straight past.

## Districts (starting palette)

Invent more freely, then add them to this list so they stay consistent.

- **Third Canal (San-bori).** Old water district of bars and boathouses under the elevated line. Bar Kurage is said to be here.
- **Kōgai Heights.** Corporate towers. Boardrooms, lobby fountains, security guards who never blink. Banks and holding companies.
- **The Lantern Market.** Night market of food stalls and stranger things for sale. Witches and worse trade here quietly.
- **Shirokane Arcade District.** Neon, game halls, karaoke, love hotels, yakuza-adjacent nightlife.
- **The Old Shrine Hill.** Steps, torii gates, cedar trees and a view over the whole city. Quiet. Watchful.

## Money

Alice arrives with pounds sterling, which are curiosities in Yūgure at best. The local currency is the **sen**, in paper notes and holed coins. She will need to earn, trade or conjure some (and conjured money has a way of turning back into leaves).

## Magic in Yūgure

- Witches exist here and are known, if not loved. A few run shops in the Lantern Market; others work for powerful people.
- Alice's magic is playful, everyday witchcraft in the Sabrina tradition: pointing, rhymes, glamours, transformations, talking to cats. It is not dark, it is not about curses, and it has a sense of humour.
- Her powers only just woke and are still settling. They are strong but unpredictable, especially when she is upset.
