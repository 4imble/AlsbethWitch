# Case file: The disappearance of Simon

GM eyes only. The player has agreed not to read this. Never show it, quote it, or put anything from it on the sheet before Alice has found it in play.

## 1. The truth

**The Great Lantern and its Keeper.** Yūgure stays at dusk because of the **Great Lantern** on the Old Shrine Hill. Its light is what guides the city's dead on their way and keeps true night (and the lost, hungry dark that comes with it) out of the streets. Every lantern in the city is lit from it. The Great Lantern can only be kept by a living soul from the waking world: someone patient and kind, who sits up late and keeps the light on for others. When a Keeper dies, the Hoshino family, Yūgure's hereditary lantern-makers, make a **seeking lantern**, and the priest of the Shrine Hill sets it on the canal at dusk. It drifts out of the city, over a sea that isn't on any map, and washes up wherever the next Keeper is.

**Why Simon.** The last Keeper, **Edwin Lark**, a lamplighter from Brighton who vanished in 1926, died last winter after sixty years of tending the light. The seeking lantern chose Simon: a man who sits up till all hours, who makes tea without being asked, who never minds two cats in the bed. It washed up on the shingle after the storm, bone dry, and he brought it home and hung it in his window, which, in the old way of things, is saying yes without knowing it.

**The singing.** Bound to the seeking lantern is its guide-spirit, **Oharu**, a gentle yūrei in a faded blue kimono, a lantern-maker's daughter who drowned in Third Canal long ago. Her task is to sing the chosen one home along the lantern road to the Shrine Hill. She's been singing to Simon at night for weeks. The line on his laptop is her song: Simon, half asleep, started typing the words he kept hearing, and the night he left, the laptop kept typing them by itself. Written in old lantern-script, the characters stack into the shape of a lantern. Read aloud, they mean roughly *"The light is waiting. Come home by the water."*

**Who took Simon:** **Kagami Sayo**, the Hoshino family's house witch. A mirror-witch: she travels and watches through reflections. She acted alone. **Hoshino Kenji**, head of the family, doesn't know.

**How.** Sayo wants the Great Lantern for herself, and a Keeper is the key to it. A week before Simon left, she stepped out of the hallway mirror at home while the house slept, and slipped a **Bar Kurage matchbook** into his coat pocket: a token that bends any road through the nearest mirror to Kurage's back room. She bought the bending charm from Kitsune Jun. On the night itself, the lantern lit, Oharu sang, Simon rose from his chair, dazed and in his socks, and walked into the light. The lantern road should have carried him to the Shrine Hill. The token pulled him sideways, through the dark study window glass, into the back-room mirror of **Bar Kurage**, 3rd Canal Street, at about 7:40 p.m. Two of Sayo's men in grey raincoats were waiting and took him by boat along the canal to Hoshino Tower. Oharu was left behind in the empty lantern, still singing to a man who isn't there.

**Where he is now.** The **Hall of Unlit Lanterns** beneath Hoshino Tower, Kōgai Heights: a vast cellar where the Hoshinos store thousands of paper lanterns, all dark, hanging like sleeping bats. Simon is kept in a lantern-maker's workroom off the hall. He's fed, warm and unhurt, but under a **forgetting glamour** that thickens each day, and Sayo is slowly drawing his warmth into a **mirror-lantern** of her own making: a lantern of silvered glass that will burn with a Keeper's light and answer only to her. Part of Simon knows something is wrong. Every midnight he folds a little paper lantern from the workroom scraps, writes one word on it, and sets it on the drain that runs out to Third Canal. (Day 2: `ALICE`. Day 3: `UNDER`. Day 4: `TOWER`. Day 5: `CATS?` Then they stop.)

**Sayo's plan.** On the seventh night, the **Long Dusk**, the Great Lantern, with no Keeper, will gutter out. Sayo means to light her mirror-lantern on the Shrine Hill in its place. Then the city's light is hers: she decides which streets stay in dusk and which go dark, starting with the Lantern Market, whose witches once laughed her out.

**What can save him.** Breaking the glamour needs something of his true life: Alice herself, his reading glasses, his name spoken by someone who loves him, or the cats (he'd never forget the cats). His name is the hook for the locked power "The name that answers". Oharu, reunited with Simon, can still sing him on to the Shrine Hill. Whether he becomes Keeper, comes home, or something in between (Keeping from the south coast, through the garden door?) is a choice for the end of the story, and Alice and Simon's to make.

## 3. Suspects

**Kagami Sayo** (the truth). The Hoshinos' house witch. Elegant, cool, always carries a silver compact mirror; a thin silver ring on her left thumb. Motive: the Great Lantern, power over the city, and revenge on the Lantern Market witches. Secret: she took the Keeper and means to take the light. Alibi: "At the Hoshino family's banquet at the Tsuru Hotel all evening." Half true: she left the dining room for "about two hours" and stepped through the powder-room mirror. Lies about: ever having been to the other side; the seeking lantern; knowing Simon's name.

**Hoshino Kenji.** Head of the Hoshino lantern-makers, sixties, tired, decent in a cowardly way. Motive (apparent): the lantern Simon found is Hoshino-made (the star mark on its base). Secret: he knows the Great Lantern is failing and the outer districts are going dark, and he's hiding it to keep the family's name; his own hand made the seeking lantern, and he's terrified it failed. Alibi: the banquet, solid. Lies about: the Great Lantern, the outer districts. Genuinely doesn't know about Simon; Insight shows real confusion, then grief. Once he learns what Sayo is doing, he becomes an ally (keys to the Hall, the family's old lantern-lore).

**Ume, "Mama" of Bar Kurage.** Old woman behind the bar, jellyfish tattoo on her wrist, sings badly to city pop. A retired witch; she once knew Alice's aunts (she'll recognise the family face). Secret: she saw Simon come through the mirror and the men take him, and took Sayo's money to stay quiet because she's afraid of her. Alibi: in the bar all night (true). Lies about: having seen anyone come through. Crack her with kindness, the aunts, or the cats, not threats.

**Inspector Tachibana Gorō.** Yūgure city police, trench coat, nicotine gum, a good detective gone soft. Motive (apparent): he warns Alice off hard. Secret: he keeps the outer districts' disappearances off the books as a favour to the Hoshinos, and runs errands for Sayo. Lies about: who sent him. He's ashamed, and his own sister went unlit; a well-placed truth spell or the right evidence turns him.

**Kitsune Jun.** Fox-masked fixer in the Lantern Market, sells charms, sigils and secrets. Motive (apparent): he sold the bending charm. Secret: yes he did, to "a woman with a mirror for a face" (she spoke to him through a compact), and he knows it bends a spirit road. Alibi: at his stall. Lies about: the buyer, at first; and from Day 3 he's paid to point Alice to Shirokane. Loves a trade; respects a clever witch.

**Walter Gant** (south coast). Old beachcomber with a metal detector and a flask, walks the shingle at dawn. Motive (apparent): he was on the beach the morning after the storm, saw Simon pick up the lantern, and goes shifty when asked. Secret: the same storm washed up a crate of something from a wreck that he's quietly been selling; he thinks Alice is asking about that. He also saw, and tries to forget, a lantern drifting in *against* the wind, lit, and a woman in grey standing in a rock pool's reflection, watching Simon's house. Lies about: the crate, and what he saw.

## 4. Clue map

Each conclusion has at least three routes. Looking in the right place sensibly finds the core clue; rolls add detail, speed and complications.

**A. Simon didn't walk out; the lantern called him away.**
- The lantern in the study window: Touchstone gives a flood of longing and a woman's song, and Simon's hand last of all. Witch's Sight: it's faintly warm, and a thread of light runs from it out of the window toward the garden wall.
- Oharu: at night (or if Alice sits with the lantern in the dark), the singing starts. Patience, kindness or a spell lets Alice see and speak to her. She's confused and sad: "I sang him home, but he didn't arrive."
- The laptop line: the spell book has a page on *calling-songs* and *seeking lanterns* in Aunt Prue's hand; Arcana or comparing the characters with the lantern's own printing shows they're the same script.
- The cats at the garden wall: the lantern road's door is there.
- Mrs Doreen Hart next door: saw the study window "light up like a lantern" at twenty to eight, heard singing, and no one left by the front.
- The local library or Aunt Prue: a 1926 newspaper clipping, *"Brighton Lamplighter Vanishes: Strange Lantern Found In His Window."*

**B. The lantern came from Yūgure to choose a Keeper.**
- The lantern's base: a maker's mark, a five-pointed star in a circle, and the word 星野 (Hoshino). Alice can't read it yet, but in Yūgure it's everywhere.
- Aunt Prue on the phone: "They send a lantern when the old Keeper dies. Our grandmother used to say so. I thought it was a story."
- Oharu, once trusting: the Great Lantern, the Shrine Hill, the Keeper.
- In Yūgure: Old Hamada at the shrine; Edwin Lark's ghost; "the Hoshinos light the lanterns" is common knowledge.

**C. A mirror-witch pulled him sideways.**
- The hallway mirror at home: Witch's Sight shows a fingerprint of frost in one corner that never melts.
- The matchbook, Touchstone: the strongest feeling is cold satisfaction, and the last hand isn't Simon's: a woman's, with a thin silver ring on the thumb.
- Mirror Sight in the dark study window: Simon walking blank-eyed into the light, and the glass going silver at the last moment, a woman's face in it.
- Oharu: "A cold light took him. It came from a mirror."
- Bar Kurage's back-room mirror has a rime of frost that never melts; Witch's Sight shows a door.
- Ume, once she talks: "She came out of my mirror like stepping out of a bath." Jun: "A woman with a mirror for a face." Walter Gant: the woman in the rock pool.

**D. It's Sayo, not Kenji.**
- Kenji's real confusion and grief; his fear is about the Great Lantern.
- Tsuru Hotel staff: Sayo left the banquet for two hours; Mirror Sight in the powder-room mirror shows her stepping through.
- Sayo's silver thumb ring and compact, seen in person.
- Tachibana, turned: "It's her calls I take, not his."
- Sayo's rooms at the top of Hoshino Tower: drawings of a silvered-glass lantern, and a map of the city with the Lantern Market circled in red.

**E. Simon is under Hoshino Tower, in the Hall of Unlit Lanterns.**
- The little paper lanterns floating down Third Canal at midnight, each with a word in Simon's handwriting. Ume's ghostly piano player has seen them; the cats will fish one out.
- Kenji, or the family's keys and plans.
- Jun knows the old drain tunnel from the San-bori boathouses to the Tower's cellars.
- Cat's Eyes: a cat can squeeze through the drain grate and see Simon among the dark lanterns.
- Oharu: if Alice carries the seeking lantern near the Tower, it tugs like a dowsing rod.
- "The name that answers" (unlock when someone says Simon's name near the Tower): the pull points down.

## 5. Red herrings

1. **Walter Gant's shiftiness.** Honest explanation: the wreck crate he's been selling. A small crook, not a kidnapper.
2. **Inspector Tachibana warning Alice off.** He protects the Hoshinos' silence about the dark districts. He thinks Alice is a meddling outsider.
3. **The unlit districts and their ghosts.** Real and eerie, but caused by the Great Lantern failing without a Keeper, not by a kidnapper. (They matter to the finale: give the Great Lantern its Keeper's light again and the dark goes away.)
4. **Shirokane Arcade lead** (Day 3+, Jun's paid lie): a gangster's karaoke box where a "foreigner who sings" turns up nightly. He's a local crooner who calls himself Simon Tanabe.

## 6. Places and NPCs

**South coast**
- **Home.** Terraced house near the sea. The study: the paper lantern in the window, Simon's laptop with the line repeating, his gaming PC and half-finished projects, his tea in his unchipped green frog mug. The loft: a box with Alice's old things (the spell book is in her satchel already, but the box holds an aunt's letter about lanterns). The hallway mirror. The garden wall: the door.
- **Mrs Doreen Hart.** Next door, seventies, nosy, kind, feeds the cats biscuits she shouldn't.
- **PC Dawson.** Local police on the phone; polite, unhurried, thinks Simon walked out.
- **Aunt Prue.** Alice's surviving aunt, Cornwall, answers the phone after nine rings. Knows more than she ever said. ("So it's come, then.")
- **The beach.** Shingle, groynes, rock pools, the pier lights through fog. Where the lantern washed up. Walter Gant walks it at dawn.

**Yūgure**
- **Bar Kurage**, 3rd Canal Street, San-bori. A boathouse bar under the elevated line; glowing jellyfish in a tank behind the bottles; the back-room mirror is where the door comes out. Ume runs it.
- **Hoshino Tower**, Kōgai Heights. Black glass, a lobby fountain with lanterns floating in it. Kenji's rooms near the top; Sayo's rooms at the very top; the Hall of Unlit Lanterns beneath the cellars.
- **The Tsuru Hotel**, Kōgai Heights. Where the banquet was. Gold and red, a powder room of mirrors.
- **The Lantern Market.** Jun's stall: masks, charms, sigils folded into paper cranes.
- **The Old Shrine Hill.** The Great Lantern in its shrine, flickering lower each night. The priest, **Old Hamada**, set the seeking lantern on the water and is waiting for a Keeper who never came.
- **The outer districts** (e.g. **Kurayami-chō**). Streets where the lanterns flicker and fail; shuttered doors; people leave tea out for neighbours who went unlit.

## 6b. Ghosts and the uncanny

Ghosts and spooky beats are welcome in every chapter (atmosphere, not gore).
- **Oharu**, the seeking lantern's guide-spirit. Blue kimono, wet hair that never dries, a lovely sad voice. Gentle, lonely, a little lost in time. A possible companion if Alice takes the lantern with her.
- **Edwin Lark**, the last Keeper, lingers at the Shrine Hill. Kindly, sounds like a grandad from Hove, calls everyone "duck". He knows what Keeping is, what it costs and what it gives, and that it doesn't have to mean never going home.
- **The unlit.** People taken by the dark in the outer districts linger as pale ghosts, walking their old routes, knocking at their own doors. They can be spoken to with patience. **Mrs Endō** of Kurayami-chō saw grey raincoats carrying a man in socks along the canal.
- **Bar Kurage's drowned piano player**, a quiet ghost who plays one song at 2 a.m. and saw everything that came through the mirror.
- **The house on the south coast** has an old, harmless presence in the loft (a great-grandmother witch? Aunt Prue would know) that knocks when Alice is near something important.
- Ghosts never threaten the cats. The cats can see ghosts: Saskee (friendly, a bit of a scaredy-cat) hides behind Alice's legs; Suki couldn't care less. Both adore Simon and treat Alice as the spare.
