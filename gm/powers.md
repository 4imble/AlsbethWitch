# Alice's powers (GM reference)

Alice starts play as an ordinary person: every magical power below is `locked` on the sheet with a teaser description. When the story wakes one (her first time using it, or a clear moment of discovery), unlock it on the sheet: set `status` to `active` and copy the `name`, `cost` and `description` from here. Keep this file as the source of truth for what each power does; adjust wording if play has shaped it.

When her first costed power wakes, set `witchlight` to 4/4 (it stays 0/0 until then, and the sheet shows "Not yet woken").

## Point and Wish

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** Free
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Point a finger and nudge the world a little: float a key across a room, flick a lock open, light a lamp, tidy a mess. Small things only, and they don't always land quite where she meant.

## Rhyme Spells

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** 1 witchlight
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Say a little rhyme and point, and it happens: a teacup turns into a mouse, a door that wasn't there is, a nosy stranger forgets what he saw. Bigger wishes need better rhymes, and magic takes the words very literally.

## Glamour

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** 1 witchlight
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Change her look with a twirl: a new outfit, hair, face, even age, for a scene. Good for slipping into places Alice shouldn't be. It doesn't change her voice.

## Mirror Sight

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** 1 witchlight
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Look into a reflection at a place (a mirror, a shop window, a puddle full of neon) and glimpse something that happened there within the last day. Clearer with something that belonged to the person she's looking for.

## Touchstone

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** Free
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Hold an object and feel the strongest feeling left on it, and a flash of the last hand that held it.

## Cat's Eyes

- **Kind:** bond
- **Cost:** 1 witchlight
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Close her eyes and see, for a few minutes, through Saskee's or Suki's. The cats go where Alice can't, and they always find their way back to her.

## Witch's Sight

- **Kind:** talent
- **Cost:** —
- **Wakes:** early, during Chapter One as she discovers it

Since the night Simon vanished, Alice notices what's hidden by magic: glamours, wards, and doors other people walk straight past.

## Sharp Eye

- **Kind:** talent
- **Cost:** —
- **Wakes:** already active. It's an ordinary talent, not magic, so it stays on the sheet from the start

When numbers, times or stories don't add up, Alice feels it before she can say why.

## Stop the clock

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** —
- **Wakes:** later, at a milestone. Only a teaser exists; invent its real name, cost and effect when it wakes

Once, when a glass slipped from her hand, it hung in the air for a heartbeat before it fell.

## Cats who talk back

- **Kind:** bond
- **Cost:** —
- **Wakes:** later, at a milestone. Only a teaser exists; invent its real name, cost and effect when it wakes

Lately Suki looks at her as if she has a great deal to say.

## The name that answers

- **Kind:** power
- **Cost:** —
- **Wakes:** later, at a milestone. Only a teaser exists; invent its real name, cost and effect when it wakes

Something stirs whenever a stranger says Simon's name.
